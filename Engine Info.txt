Silent Hill (PS1) Engine Architecture, Subsystems, and Data Formats
===================================================================

1. High-Level Engine Architecture & Execution Model
---------------------------------------------------
Due to the PS1's strict 2 MB RAM constraint, the engine is structured as a modular overlay system:

- Main Executable (SLUS_007.07 / main):
  * Acts as the primary bootloader.
  * Displays the content warning screen and initializes hardware.
  * Embeds the Master File Table (filetable.c.USA.inc) which indexes all resources across the disc containers.
  * Loads B_KONAMI.BIN and BODYPROG.BIN.

- Main Game Logic Overlay (BODYPROG.BIN):
  * Resides in the 1ST/ folder of the SILENT. container (encrypted with an XOR key).
  * Houses the core engine: main loop, character physics, rendering pipeline, camera systems, collision engine, and inventory.

- Transient Overlays (VIN/ folder):
  * Dynamically swapped in and out of RAM depending on context:
    - MAP#_S##.BIN: Level-specific event scripts and enemy AI logic.
    - STREAM.BIN: FMV video playback handler.
    - OPTION.BIN: Options menu logic and graphics.
    - SAVELOAD.BIN: Save and load screen UI and logic.
    - STF_ROLL.BIN: Ending credits sequence.

- Game State Machines:
  * e_GameState (Application Flow):
    GameState_Init, GameState_KonamiLogo, GameState_MainMenu, GameState_InGame,
    GameState_InventoryScreen, GameState_PaperMapScreen, GameState_SaveScreen,
    GameState_Credits, GameState_MovieOpening, etc.
  * e_SysState (In-Game Tick Loop):
    SysState_Gameplay, SysState_OptionsMenu, SysState_StatusMenu, SysState_MapScreen,
    SysState_Fmv, SysState_LoadOverlay, SysState_LoadRoom, SysState_ReadMessage,
    SysState_GameOver, SysState_GamePaused.


2. Core Engine Subsystems & Functions
-------------------------------------

A. Graphics, Dynamic Lighting & Fog (Gfx_*, s_MapEffectsInfo):
- Volumetric Fog & Darkness:
  * The PS1 GPU lacks hardware fog. The engine relies on GTE Depth Cueing (DPCS/DPCT)
    to blend vertex colors toward a target fog color (fogColor) or pitch black based
    on depth distance (fogDistance).
  * Environment presets dynamically blend between outdoor fog and indoor blackness.
- Flashlight & Spotlights:
  * Uses a dual-preset model in s_SysWork_2388: separate lighting configurations for
    flashlight ON vs. OFF, allowing smooth runtime cross-fading.
  * Real-time light cone attenuation projected onto level geometry and character meshes.
- Weather & Particle System (Particle_SystemUpdate):
  * Dedicated particle engine driving town snowfall, rain, and ash effects.

B. Camera System (vc_* and vw_*):
- Camera Rails (VC_ROAD_DATA):
  * Cinematic fixed camera perspectives in alleys and corridors follow pre-authored spline paths/rails (vcInitVCSystem).
- Dynamic Following (vcUserCamTarget, vcUserWatchTarget):
  * Smoothly transitions to third-person behind-the-shoulder tracking in open streets.
- Cutscene Bone Tracking (Vc_SetLookAtMatFromBoneCoord):
  * Anchors camera translation and view rotation to specific character skeleton bones during scripted cutscenes.

C. Physics & Collision (collision/, ipd.h):
- Subcell Partitioning:
  * Level collision is stored in .IPD files broken into a 2D grid of subcells (s_IpdCollSubcell).
  * Each subcell contains split vertices and surfaces (s_IpdCollSurface).
- Ground Types (e_GroundType):
  * Surfaces are tagged with materials (GroundType_Default, Grass, Wood, Concrete, etc.)
    which dictate Harry's footstep sound effects.
- Slopes & Stairs:
  * Collision surfaces store pitch and tilt angles (tiltAngleX, tiltAngleZ) enabling smooth stair and hill traversal.
- Line-of-Sight & Raycasting (los.h, ray.h):
  * Drives projectile hit detection, enemy vision cones, and item pickup triggers.

D. Characters & NPC Management (s_SubCharacter, s_PlayerWork):
- Memory Caps:
  * Maximum of 4 distinct character archetypes (CHARA_GROUP_COUNT = 4) in RAM simultaneously.
  * Maximum of 6 active NPCs (NPC_COUNT_MAX = 6) instantiated in the world.
- Bone Hierarchy & Animation Blending:
  * Built on GsCOORDINATE2 trees.
  * Harry's animation model (s_PlayerExtra) splits animations at the waist: upper body
    (aiming, shooting, lighting matches) operates independently from lower body locomotion
    (running, turning) via disabledAnimBones bitmasks.

E. Audio & Radio System:
- Konami libsd / libsmf Sound Driver:
  * Custom sound system used across Konami PS1 and early PS2 titles (directly related to Silent Hill 2's SOUNDCD.IRX).
- Dynamic Music Layering (Bgm_LayersUpdate):
  * Multi-layer ambient music tracks that fade individual stems in and out based on tension and enemy presence.
- Radio Static (radio.h):
  * Polling routines calculate distance to nearby monsters and dynamically inject analog radio noise.


3. File Formats & Asset Architecture
------------------------------------
All assets are consolidated into two large container files without internal directory headers:
- SILENT.: Overlays, 3D models, textures, animations, scripts.
- HILL.: Streaming CD-XA audio tracks and MDEC FMV cinematics.
- Master File Table: Resides in the main executable, mapping resource IDs to LBA sector offsets.

File Types:
- .ILM: Skeletal 3D models for characters and monsters (with bone weighting).
- .IPD: Local map geometry (room meshes, street chunks, and subcell collision data).
- .PLM: Global static props (pickups, keys, interactive environment items).
- .TMD: Standard PS1 3D model format (used exclusively on the 3D item inspection screen).
- .ANM: Skeletal joint rotation animation keyframes.
- .DMS: Scripted cutscene keyframe data (actor choreography and camera cues).
- .TIM: Standard PS1 textures (4-bit/8-bit indexed with CLUTs or 15-bit direct color).
- .KDT: Konami MIDI tracker sequence files.
- .VAB: SPU instrument sample banks and SFX waveforms.
- .STR / XA: CD-XA interleaved audio streams and MDEC FMV video.


4. Mathematical Data Representations
------------------------------------
Lacking a hardware FPU, the engine uses fixed-point math throughout:
- Q19.12 (32-bit, 12 fractional bits):
  * Primary world coordinate unit for positions, velocities, and health (playerHealth defaults to Q12(100.0f)).
- Q3.12 (16-bit, 12 fractional bits):
  * Unit vectors, rotation matrices, normalized angles, and lighting/fog intensity.
- Q7.8 (16-bit, 8 fractional bits):
  * Local .IPD collision cell offsets and dimensions.
- Q20.12:
  * Timers and accumulators (gameplay timer, total running/walking distance).


5. Save Data & Game Progress State (s_Savegame)
-----------------------------------------------
The save structure occupies an 8,192-byte block (1 PS1 memory card block):
- Inventory: Array of equipped weapons, item counts, and toggle states (s_InventoryItem items[INV_ITEM_COUNT_MAX]).
- Map & World Progress:
  * mapIdx, mapRoomIdx, locationId.
  * mapEnemyStates[45]: Bitfield tracking whether individual enemies have been permanently killed or just incapacitated.
  * eventFlags[52]: 1,664 individual event bit flags tracking story progression, puzzle solutions, unlocked doors, and paper map markings.
- Stat Tracking & Endings:
  * Shot statistics (firedShotCount, closeRangeShotCount, midRangeShotCount, longRangeShotCount).
  * Melee and ranged kill counts.
  * Running and walking distances.
  * Ending unlock flags (Good+, Good, Bad+, Bad, UFO).
  * Known Original Bug: pickedUpSpecialItemCount packs 3 bits for item count and 2 bits for Hyper Blaster beam color, causing the results screen to miscalculate special item counts.
