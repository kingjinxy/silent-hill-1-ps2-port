# Profiles, 2026-11-03

Figures are thousands of EE cycles per frame, averaged over the light-counter reports (`SH1_LITEPROF=1`, one
report per 30 frames). 1,000k ≈ 3.4 ms at 294.912 MHz. PCSX2 doesn't model the EE caches or GS waits, so these
are close to instruction counts; console figures for the same scenes are higher (see the notes).

Purpose: the go/no-go split for VU1 packet expansion (Graphics Pipeline Rework.md, step 5). VU1 would replace
the emit part only; parse and state stay on the EE.

**Notes:**
- Emit, the part VU1 would take over, is 16% of DrawOTag in both scenes at 2x/32-bit (619k, 138k); state is
  45% and 42%. At 1x in Kaufmann emit is 26% (899k, of which `blend_exact` 322k).
- VU1 saves at most emit minus the cost of writing records: about 0.3-0.5M a frame in Kaufmann and under 0.1M
  in the Docks, by these figures.
- The console's DrawOTag is much higher (2026-10-28 Runs 2 and 4, `SH1_PROF`): Kaufmann 5,992 against 3,794
  here, Docks 3,195 against 866. Where that extra goes (cache misses, GS waits, the profiler's own cost) isn't
  known; a console `SH1_LITEPROF` run of the same two scenes would show whether emit's share changes.
